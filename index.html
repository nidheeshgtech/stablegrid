About StableGrid

A needs-driven model for stronger and more resilient power grids.

A scalable infrastructure model designed to strengthen Africa’s power grids through targeted investment in distributed energy storage and solar generation.

StableGrid works with utilities and energy-sector stakeholders to identify the parts of transmission and distribution networks under the greatest strain, then deploys targeted solar PV and battery storage where they can create the most value.

  2. battery_charging_full 02

    Deploy

  3. trending_down 03

    Deliver

    Reduce technical losses, improve grid performance and defer or substitute conventional upgrades.

  4. handshake 04

HOW IT WORKS

A needs-driven model for grid stabilization.

StableGrid uses a data and needs-driven approach to identify priority grid nodes, deploy targeted infrastructure and create scalable value across transmission and distribution networks.

analytics 01
Step 01

Data & Needs-Driven Approach

StableGrid begins from the perspective of the transmission and distribution network. Priority nodes are identified where the grid is under strain because of high local energy demand, distance from generation points, inadequate voltage lines or other infrastructure constraints.

Grid Diagnostics & Telemetry
bolt 02
Step 02

Targeted Grid Stabilization

StableGrid develops a detailed needs-driven design to stabilize priority grid nodes. Solar PV and battery storage are deployed close to identified substations and demand nodes, based on the capacities and priorities scheduled by the transmission system operator and distribution company.

Node-Level Deployment
trending_up 03
Step 03

CAPEX & OPEX Value Creation

From a transmission and distribution perspective, targeted StableGrid infrastructure can enable CAPEX deferral or substitution. Reducing technical losses can also create OPEX value while improving network performance.

Loss Reduction & Deferral
account_balance 04
Step 04

Investment Structure

StableGrid works closely with key energy-sector stakeholders including Ministries of Energy, TSOs, DistCos, National Utilities, and Regulators to structure commercially viable infrastructure investments while maintaining appropriate risk-weighted returns.

Public & Private Finance
payments 05
Step 05

Cost-Reflective Pricing

StableGrid uses an open-book data-sharing approach with energy-sector stakeholders to support lowest-cost-of-service balancing, exploring electricity tariffs and grid stabilization charges.

Transparent Tariff Models
hub 06
Step 06

Country-Wide Scalability

StableGrid is designed to begin with key areas of grid need, scaling across additional substations and demand nodes according to utility and network priorities.

Modular Expansion
